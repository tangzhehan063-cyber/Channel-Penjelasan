# Kata kunci mencari referensi moodboard (Google, YouTube, Pinterest, Behance, Dribbble)
Tambahkan `shorts` di YouTube untuk format vertikal; tambahkan `moodboard` / `style frames` di Pinterest/Behance.
- **Gaya visual:** `soft 3D illustration`, `clean 3D render flat color`, `papercraft 3D diorama`, `paper cut 3D animation`, `3D editorial illustration`, `minimal 3D motion graphics`
- **Rumah dan benda:** `isometric house cutaway`, `dollhouse view`, `exploded view animation`, `architectural section animation`, `cross section animation`, `isometric room 3D`, `3D explainer animation how it works`
- **Kamera:** `orthographic camera 3D animation`, `camera push in macro 3D`, `motion design transitions camera dive`
- **Teks/kartu:** `kinetic typography explainer`, `short form video captions style`, `lower third card design`
Aturan memakai referensi dari user: acuan penempatan dan suasana, bukan ditiru persis atau dirender realistis. Simpan gambar di `referensi/moodboard/` dengan catatan 1 baris: bagian mana yang disukai (warna, bentuk, kamera, tata letak).
